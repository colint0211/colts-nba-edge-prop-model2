Model methodology and safeguards
  • Independent home and away score projections from opponent-adjusted offensive/defensive efficiency and pace.
  • Training-only historical bias correction and residual standard deviation; the untouched holdout is an eligibility audit, never a source of fitted coefficients.
  • Normal distribution for game totals with integer-line push probability handled explicitly.
  • DraftKings main full-game total only. No spreads, moneylines, player props or alternate totals.
  • Two-sided no-vig market probability is a comparison benchmark only. Model probability remains independent.
  • Minimum 3% expected value, fresh odds feed and no unresolved questionable/doubtful player availability.
  • Daily game projections and official picks are frozen once. Settlement uses final scores only.
  • Counterfactual factor impacts are explanatory model diagnostics, not causal effects.
